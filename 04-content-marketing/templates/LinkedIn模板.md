# LinkedIn 帖子模板

## 特点
- 语言：英文为主，或中英混合
- 风格：专业、有深度、有观点
- 开头要有hook（前2行决定是否点开「See more」）
- 适当使用段落留白增加可读性
- 字数：500-1500字符

---

## 模板A：行业洞察型

> **Hook**: One question I get asked the most:
>
> "Do I really need insurance if I'm young and healthy?"
>
> Here's what I tell them:
>
> Insurance isn't about today. It's about protecting your future self — the one who might have a family, a mortgage, and bigger responsibilities.
>
> [3-4 paragraphs of insights]
>
> As a Financial Planner at AIA, I've seen firsthand how proper planning transforms lives.
>
> What's your take? Drop a comment below. 👇
>
> #FinancialPlanning #Insurance #AIA #WealthManagement #HongKong

---

## 模板B：个人成就/里程碑

> **Hook**: Grateful to announce that [achievement]. 🎉
>
> [X] years ago, I started my journey in financial planning with zero experience in the industry.
>
> Today, I'm proud to have:
> • Helped [XX]+ families build their financial safety net
> • Built a team of [XX] dedicated financial planners
> • Achieved [MDRT/COT/TOT] for [X] consecutive years
>
> The biggest lesson? Success in this industry isn't about selling — it's about truly caring for your clients' financial well-being.
>
> To my clients: Thank you for your trust.
> To my team: Thank you for your dedication.
>
> Here's to the next chapter. 🚀
>
> #CareerMilestone #FinancialPlanning #AIA #MDRT #Leadership

---

## 模板C：思考型/观点型

> **Hook**: Unpopular opinion: Most people don't need more insurance. They need BETTER insurance.
>
> [Your perspective with reasoning]
>
> What do you think? Agree or disagree?
>
> #Insurance #FinancialLiteracy #HongKong #Perspective

## 模板D：招募型

> **Hook**: Looking for your next career move? Here's why financial planning might be it.
>
> 3 things I wish I knew before joining the industry:
>
> 1. **It's not about selling** — It's about solving problems
> 2. **There's no income ceiling** — Your effort determines your reward
> 3. **You genuinely help people** — And that's incredibly fulfilling
>
> If you're curious about a career at AIA, feel free to reach out. No pressure, just a conversation. ☕
>
> #CareerChange #FinancialPlanning #AIA #Hiring #HongKong
